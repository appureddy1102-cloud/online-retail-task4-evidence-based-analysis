## Documented transformations

Derived CSVs, tables and charts were produced by T. Aparna Reddy for this revised Task 4 analysis. Changes include removal of 5,268 exact duplicate rows under an explicitly disclosed assumption; source-row trace identifiers; lossless identifier/datetime normalization; an UNKNOWN missing-description sentinel; edge-whitespace trimming; derived flags, signed values and time dimensions; and separately named sales/merchandise/customer analytical views. Credits, anonymous records and legitimate outliers are preserved in the complete cleaned dataset. Full code and before/after evidence are supplied.
